This is the first entry in what I hope becomes a long-running research log.

I started research in the Foster Lab in January 2025, and so far most of what I've learned
lives in scattered lab notebooks, Slack threads, and my own head. The goal of this site —
and especially these notes — is to think in the open: to write down what I tried, what broke,
and what I'd do differently.

A few things I want to use this space for:

- **Experiment post-mortems.** What I expected, what actually happened with the maize
  kinesin-14 expression, and the troubleshooting in between.
- **Paper notes.** Short summaries of papers I read for the active-matter and
  motor-transport literature.
- **Modeling diaries.** Notes from the Gillespie/SSA work — assumptions, parameter
  choices, and sanity checks.

If you're reading this and want to talk about active matter, kinesin motors, or quantitative
biology, [email me](mailto:shenaugu@usc.edu).

> Tip to future me: write the entry the same day. The details fade fast.
